# Code of conduct

We want bydawn-dryrun to be a calm, useful place to work, for everyone. We adopt the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as our code of conduct, and it applies in every part of this project: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews.

## In short

- Be kind and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Be specific. Say what you saw and what you expected.
- No harassment, insults, or sexual language or imagery, and no publishing other people's private information.

## Reporting a problem

If someone's behaviour here worries you, tell the maintainers privately, on GitHub:

- Open the **Security** tab of this repository and choose **Report a vulnerability** ([direct link](https://github.com/LenderCom/bydawn-dryrun/security/advisories/new)). Start the title with `Conduct:`. Only the maintainers can read it.
- For a single comment, you can also use GitHub's own **Report content** in the comment's menu.

We'll reply within three working days, and we won't share who reported it.

## Enforcement

The maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines: a private correction, a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban, depending on what happened.
